Watched this for the Miles Davis score, but the rest of this 1950s French crime thriller is just as stylized. This movie features some stunning live street photography heralding the New Wave, a young brooding Jeanne Moreau (*Orwell's The Trial, La Piscine*) and a love story much more palpable than that of Hollywood noirs.

The highlight for me is still the Miles Davis soundtrack. The Criterion Channel has a 5 minute cut of Davis solo, standing, playing his trumpet in front of a projection of the film, smoke literally coming off his fingertips. Apparently he improvised the score live upon seeing scenes from the film, which reinforces the “cool” while adding an off-kilter vibe to Malle’s otherwise curated atmosphere.

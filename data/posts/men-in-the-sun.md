Men in the Sun is dripping with the shame and anger Kanafani experienced as a young boy displaced from his family home in the 1948 Nakba. He writes in visceral physical detail, using the relentless heat to reflect the sense of powerlessness that defines the Palestinian condition. Kanafani isn’t an explicitly political writer in the sense that he only depicts the psychological impacts to the individual. However, his novella humanizes an otherwise (to me) alien feeling: the personal reality of what it is to be without home, to be robbed of your hope and future and be unable to do anything about it.

As I walk home, it all seems vast;
The moon light pouring down on me
Wash everything in soaking gray:
Pristine and clinical

The doting trees we used to climb
Now tower, unapproachable
What changed so much I cannot find
In branches dark and full

The dining hall stands like a tomb
Of mourning, impassivity
Or rock slab temple on the moon;
I tread on hallowed ground

I used to skate on red brick tiles
Clickety clack up to your door
But now I walk the many miles,
Hands empty, nothing for

So in the night it all seems vast,
They used to watch and comfort me,
Behemoths once familiar,
Now turn their face from me

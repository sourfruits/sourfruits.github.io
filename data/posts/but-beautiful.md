A reverent and poetic collection of semi-biographical vignettes on nine jazz legends. Drawing from historical facts and photographs, Dyer takes details of personality and performance and breathes life into his vision of each player. The prose in each chapter reflects the playing styles of their musical inspirations. Pres’s section is lilting, affectionate, characterized by flowing phrases that fall into the next, while in Monk’s, paragraphs lurch and stutter, punctuated by short phrases. These stories are bookended by essays — his Sontagian thoughts on photography and a meditation on improvisation, interpretation, and all art as criticism. This book tickled so many parts of my brain, and I really enjoyed it.
